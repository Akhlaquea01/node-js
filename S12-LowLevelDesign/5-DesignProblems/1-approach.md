### Low level design (50 Mintues)
* Don't cram solutions
* Looks for structure
* Expectation is not to come up with a working solution rather than engage in meaningful dialogue


## Structure / Stage

### 1. Requirement Gathering (5-10)
- Focus the time for asking questions from different aspect
- Stating Assumptions and confirming it from the interviewer
- Confirm with the interviewer that the requirements are locked

- Functional Requirement
    - Park a vehicle
    - Unpark a vechicl
    - Process Payment
- Non Functional requirement
    - Transactions should succeed in less than 1 sec
    - Scalable 
    - Modular


### 2. Identify System Entities (5 minutes)
- Nouns of the system (10-15)
- Correlate it with all the agreed requirements
- Try to identify the flow and work in and around it


### 3. Detail Design of the system (30 minutes)
- Go entity by entity
- Identify the attributes of the entity
- Identify the behaviours of the entity
- Identify relationship between the entities

- Where to start? 
    - Top to Bottom
        - Start with the biggest class and figure out the related entities
    - Bottom to Top
        - Start with smallest ones and then build around it
